About Dev Agarwalla

DevShipIt is Dev Agarwalla, a full-stack and applied AI engineer in Bengaluru. Every DevShipIt project is built by him personally.

Who builds your project

Dev is a Technical Delivery Associate at Falcon Reality, an AI product company, where he turns operations workflows into product specs and MVP scope. Before that he shipped three MERN applications to production as a full-stack intern at Cyber Secure India. On his own he has built and shipped four products: JantaHR, RentWise, linkedin-mcp and ISP Evidence.

What DevShipIt does

  • Freelance: MERN-stack websites and web apps for property agents and agencies in Bangalore that list properties, capture enquiries and are easy to keep up to date. Every project is priced by a written quote before work starts. See freelance services.
  • Products: software Dev builds and runs himself, starting with JantaHR. See all work.
