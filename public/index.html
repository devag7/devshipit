What is DevShipIt, and how is it related to JantaHR?

DevShipIt is the trading name of Dev Agarwalla, a full-stack developer in Bengaluru, and this is its website. JantaHR is HR and payroll software for Indian companies that DevShipIt builds and sells at jantahr.devshipit.com. JantaHR case study.

Who will build my project?

Dev Agarwalla, personally. He is a Technical Delivery Associate at Falcon Reality, an AI product company, shipped three MERN applications to production as a full-stack intern at Cyber Secure India, and has built and shipped four products of his own. About Dev.

How much does a website or web app cost?

Every project is priced by a written quote with the scope, price and delivery dates, agreed before any work starts, and you pay only the amounts in that quote. Write to hello@devshipit.com with what you need, when you need it and a rough budget. See pricing.

Who owns the code and the website?

You do. For freelance work, ownership of what is built passes to you once it is paid for, unless the quote says otherwise. Read the terms.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. The delivery dates are written into your quote before any work starts, so you know them up front.
