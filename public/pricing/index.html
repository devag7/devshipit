All prices are in Indian rupees. We are not registered for GST, so no GST is added.

JantaHR: HR and payroll software

JantaHR is HR and payroll software for Indian companies, sold as a subscription at jantahr.devshipit.com. Paid plans are priced per employee seat, with a minimum of 10 seats, and are paid in advance through Razorpay.

Free₹0₹0Up to 20 employees: core HR, leave and attendance
Standard₹59 per employee a month₹49 per employee a month (₹588 a year)Payroll with PF, ESI, PT, LWF and TDS, filing files, onboarding and exits
Professional₹119 per employee a month₹99 per employee a month (₹1,188 a year)Everything in Standard plus performance, recruitment and analytics

Freelance web development

Websites and web apps for real estate agents and agencies are priced per project. You get a written quote with the scope, price and delivery dates before any work starts, and you pay only the amounts in that quote.
