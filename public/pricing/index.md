# Pricing

> Prices for JantaHR, the HR and payroll software sold by DevShipIt, and for freelance web development.

Last updated October 2026

All prices are in Indian rupees. We are not registered for GST, so no GST is added.

## JantaHR: HR and payroll software

JantaHR is HR and payroll software for Indian companies, sold as a subscription at [jantahr.devshipit.com](https://jantahr.devshipit.com). Paid plans are priced per employee seat, with a minimum of 10 seats, and are paid in advance through Razorpay.

| Plan | Billed monthly | Billed yearly | For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | ₹0 | ₹0 | Up to 20 employees: core HR, leave and attendance |
| Standard | ₹59 per employee a month | ₹49 per employee a month (₹588 a year) | Payroll with PF, ESI, PT, LWF and TDS, filing files, onboarding and exits |
| Professional | ₹119 per employee a month | ₹99 per employee a month (₹1,188 a year) | Everything in Standard plus performance, recruitment and analytics |
| Enterprise | Quoted on request | | 250 employees and more, dedicated database and SLA |

- Every new company gets a 14-day free trial of Professional. No card is needed and nothing is charged when it ends.
- Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel at any time from Billing in the app.
- Full plan details: [jantahr.devshipit.com/pricing](https://jantahr.devshipit.com/pricing).

## Freelance web development

Websites and web apps for real estate agents and agencies are priced per project. You get a written quote with the scope, price and delivery dates before any work starts, and you pay only the amounts in that quote.

Questions about prices: [jantahr@devshipit.com](mailto:jantahr@devshipit.com).
