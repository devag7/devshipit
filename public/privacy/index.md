# Privacy policy

> How DevShipIt handles personal data on devshipit.com, in JantaHR and in payments.

Last updated October 2026

DevShipIt is run by Dev Agarwalla, an individual developer based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. In these pages "we" and "us" mean Dev Agarwalla trading as DevShipIt.

## This website

devshipit.com has no sign-in, forms, cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

## When you email us

We use your name, email address and what you write only to reply to you and, if you become a customer, to provide the service. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

## JantaHR

Personal data that companies store in JantaHR (employees, payroll, attendance) is covered by the [JantaHR privacy notice](https://jantahr.devshipit.com/legal/privacy). In that product the company is the data fiduciary and we process data on its behalf. Data is stored in India.

## Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay under its own privacy policy. We receive the payment status, amount and a reference, not your card or bank details.

## Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or erase personal data we hold about you, and withdraw consent you gave. Write to us and we will respond within 30 days.
