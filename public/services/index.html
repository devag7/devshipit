How a project works

  1. Tell me what you need

    Email a few lines: what it is, when you need it and a rough budget. I reply within 2 working days.

  2. Get a written quote

    Scope, price and delivery dates, agreed before any work starts. You pay only the amounts in the quote.

  3. I build it

    I design and build what the quote describes, from the database to the interface.

  4. Delivered online

    A live website, a code repository or files, on the dates in your quote. Once paid for, it is yours.

Questions

Who will build my project?

Dev Agarwalla, personally. He is the founder of AEOLedger, an answer engine optimization (AEO) agency for B2B SaaS, shipped three MERN applications to production as a full-stack intern at Cyber Secure India, and has built and shipped four products of his own. About Dev.

How much does a website or web app cost?

Every project is priced by a written quote with the scope, price and delivery dates, agreed before any work starts, and you pay only the amounts in that quote. Write to hello@devshipit.com with what you need, when you need it and a rough budget. See pricing.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. The delivery dates are written into your quote before any work starts, so you know them up front.

Who owns the code and the website?

You do. For freelance work, ownership of what is built passes to you once it is paid for, unless the quote says otherwise. Read the terms.

How is the work delivered?

Online: a live website, a code repository or files, on the dates in your quote. Nothing is shipped physically. Shipping and delivery policy.

What if I need to cancel a project?

Cancellation and refund terms for a project, including any advance, are written into the quote you accept before work starts. Cancellation and refund policy.

What do you build with?

TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, NestJS, Express, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, Supabase, Docker and Socket.IO. Websites for property agents are usually built on the MERN stack.

How quickly do you reply?

Within 2 working days, usually sooner. Email is the fastest way to reach me. Contact.
