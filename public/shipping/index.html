Shipping and delivery policy

Last updated October 2026

DevShipIt is run by Dev Agarwalla, an individual developer based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. In these pages "we" and "us" mean Dev Agarwalla trading as DevShipIt.

Nothing is shipped

We sell software and web development services delivered over the internet. We do not sell or ship physical goods, so there are no shipping charges, couriers or delivery addresses.

When you get access

  • JantaHR trial: starts as soon as you confirm your email address.
  • JantaHR paid plans: activated automatically when Razorpay confirms the payment, normally within a few minutes. The invoice is available under Billing straight away.
  • Freelance projects: delivered online (a live website, a code repository or files) on the dates in your quote.

If access does not arrive

If money has left your account and your plan is not active within 2 hours, email jantahr@devshipit.com with the payment reference. We will activate it or refund the payment within 2 working days.

Where we sell

Our services are offered to customers in India and priced in Indian rupees.
