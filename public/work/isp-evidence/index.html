ISP Evidence

An Android app that turns "my internet keeps dropping" into evidence: it logs every drop, latency and jitter reading, then exports a PDF report you can send with a complaint.

Role
Product, design and development, solo
Started
2026
Status
In testing on Google Play (internal track)
Platform
Android

The problem

When home internet is unreliable, a complaint to the provider usually comes down to your word against theirs. A support agent asks for proof, and most people have none.

What it does

  • Logs connection drops as they happen.
  • Measures latency and jitter over time.
  • Exports a PDF report that summarizes the log, ready to attach to an ISP complaint.

Where it stands

The app is on the Google Play internal testing track while I work through feedback ahead of a wider release.

This is the first Android app I am taking to Google Play, so the release process itself is part of what I am learning here.
